## Suggested use for Belgium420

Current direction: dark Belgian flag palette (black / `#F2C200` / `#C8102E`), Anton + Inter, lab/street hybrid ecommerce.

Pull from these sources for:
- **Hero & first viewport** — Pure Landing, Godly, Landing Love (full-bleed product photography over emoji placeholders)
- **Nav / shop UX** — Navbar Gallery, Mobbin
- **Product grid & bento** — Bento Grids, Unsection, Component Gallery
- **Trust / lab / COA sections** — SaaSpo, Webinspoo (data-forward SaaS clarity)
- **Motion** — Origin Kit, App Motion, 60fps (age-gate exit, filter tabs, product hover)
- **Type & icons** — Uncut, Hugeicons (replace Inter defaults and emoji product art)
